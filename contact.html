Frequently Answered Queries

Instant answers to the most common questions from attendees and organizers.

How do I open my pass if my mobile device is offline?
All your registered passes are permanently cached in your browser’s LocalStorage. You can open Eventora or click "My Registrations" at any time even with zero cellular connectivity, and your QR code will display instantly.
Can I cancel a ticket and get my seat released?
Yes! Head to your My Registrations page, locate your ticket pass, and click "Cancel Registration". The system immediately voids your QR code and restores the seat to the public pool.
How does Google Identity Services work on Eventora?
We use the official Google Identity Services (GSI) API. When you sign in with Google, your identity is verified with cryptographic JWT tokens. Your booked tickets and passes are permanently linked to your Google profile.
What happens if the turnstile scanner fails to read my code?
Every pass features an alphanumeric Emergency Fallback Code (e.g. EVT-9041-A) directly below the barcode. Gate operators can enter this manual code on their turnstile keypad for instantaneous manual check-in.
