Organizer Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Eventora charge for paid events?
We charge a flat 2.5% platform fee on paid tickets. For community, academic, and free events, Eventora is completely 0% free. No hidden setup fees or equipment charges.
Do we need special hardware to scan QR passes?
No proprietary hardware required! Eventora provides an Organizer Gate Scanner web tool that works right in any smartphone browser camera, offline or online.
When do ticket sales payouts get deposited?
Funds from ticket bookings are settled automatically on a rolling 48-hour basis directly to your verified business bank account via IMPS/NEFT.
